# Google Ads Dashboard Analysis – Performance Max Campaign

## Campaign Overview
- **Campaign Type:** Performance Max  
- **Duration:** 3 months  
- **Goal:** Phone call leads  
- **Location:** Texas, USA  

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
- **Impressions:** 58,000  
- **CPC (Cost per Click):** $1  
- **CTR (Click Through Rate):** 8.82%  
- **Cost:** $6,020  
- **Phone Calls Generated:** 2,640  
- **CPA (Cost per Acquisition – Phone Call):** $2.28  

## Achievements & Insights
- Successfully ran a **Performance Max campaign** targeting Texas, generating a high number of **phone call leads**.  
- Achieved **excellent CTR of 8.82%**, indicating strong ad relevance and engagement.  
- Maintained **low CPC of $1** and **CPA of $2.28**, optimizing ad spend efficiently.  
- Campaign delivered **2,640 phone calls** within 3 months, demonstrating tangible lead generation results.  
- Data analysis can help identify **top-performing assets and audiences**, informing future campaign optimization and scaling decisions.
